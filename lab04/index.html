History of Dominica

Dominica is a small, ruggged island nation in the Caribbean. Often called the 'Nature Isle of the Caribbean' because of it's lush rainforests and extraordinary biodiversity. Dominica remains largely undeveloped for mass tourism, making it great for eco-tourists, hikers, and anyone who loves nature! People all across the world love Dominica for it's pristine rivers, hot springs, and the famous Boiling Lake.
