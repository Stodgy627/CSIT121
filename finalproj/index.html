About Bean & Bloom Café

Bean & Bloom Café is known for our high-quality in-house bean roasting, pastries, and calm atmosphere.

Our café is an excellent spot for reading, studying, and meeting friends. Bean & Bloom emphasizes ethically sourced beans, seasonal specialty drinks, and house-made baked goods.

Sustainability is at the heart of our business, which is why we offer rewards for reusable cups, use compostable materials, and partner with local growers & artists.
