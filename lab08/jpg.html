JPG Format Example

About This Image

This beach is on the tip of Dominica and this image shows what Dominica looks like from afar. As you can see, Dominica is a very mountainous island with beautiful views. With little modern development outside the capital, 'Roseau', Dominica definitely earns the name "The Nature Island".

Why JPG Format?

JPG is the best for this image because it can support 16.7 million colors, which is needed because of how detailed this photo is with the water, mountains, and sky included. Since JPG is best for photographs, a photo of Dominica like this one would be wasted on any other format other than JPG.
